# Actionable Books design brief

## Aesthetic direction

The Working Bookshelf: a recognisable Actionable Books library, with the existing red identity, real book covers and contributor writing doing the visual work. This is a repair of reading and discovery, preserving the established logo and archive.

## Audience and purpose

Readers looking for a useful idea from a business book, a next book to read, or a practical discussion with their team. Help them identify the book and its reviewer, read comfortably, and find a relevant next step.

## Typography

- Commissioner 400, 600 and 800 for headings, navigation and labels.
- Alegreya 400 and 600, with genuine italic, for original prose.
- Self-host Latin WOFF2 files with font-display: swap and retain OFL licences. Preload only normal faces needed above the fold.
- Reading text: 19px/1.65 desktop, 18px/1.65 phone. Summary H1: 48px desktop, 34px phone. Home H1: 64px desktop, 40px phone.

## Layout

Keep the original SVG logo with accurate alternative text. Use a compact shared header, explicit Search link and an accessible phone menu. Maximum width 1160px; prose 66ch. A thin red bookmark rule marks introductions and topic headings. Corners are 3px; no decorative shadows.

The home page opens with a short left-aligned introduction and search, followed immediately by real books and topics. Library shelves use four columns on desktop and two on phones. Titles wrap fully. Missing covers use an honest title block.

Summary pages show breadcrumb, book title, book author, and a distinct Summary by contributor credit. Original prose starts promptly, with a compact cover beside the introduction rather than a large quote hero. Contents links come from real headings. Related books follow the article. Author and contributor pages show their complete, correctly linked work.

Lists must explain specific selections and their limitations before any new affiliate expansion. Disclosures sit beside purchase links. A summary link is primary; purchase is secondary. Newsletter, paid pack and B2B offers follow the reading and require verified capture or an approved actual offer.

## Constraints

Preserve human writing, attribution, existing URLs and actual historical dates. Do not invent popularity, ratings, expertise or new publication dates. No repeated signup widgets in prose, no public Netlify Identity, no Webflow/jQuery dependency in the new shell, no unchosen ad network script, no empty reserved ad slots, no remote font stylesheet, no animated entrances, no manufactured imagery.

## References and provenance

The existing Actionable Books logo and red identity are the brand reference. The original review archive supplies the covers, portraits and writing. RogerSon's portfolio registry was checked; Commissioner and Alegreya are unused there. The read-only CMO review supplied this scoped brief.
