This past Thursday we hosted the first instalment of our **Actionable Books Live** seminars. Actionable Books founder Chris Taylor spoke to the terrific crowd at Toronto’s Books for Business about the important lessons he has gleaned interviewing the world’s leading business book authors, everyone from Seth Godin to Simon Sinek.

We thought we’d share some photos that Mijay, our Director of Partnerships & Facilitation, snapped for us!

Also, don’t forget to RSVP to our next seminar targeted towards managers and team leaders next Thursday, October 20, again at Books for Business. Click [here](http://awinfosessionb4b-eorg.eventbrite.com/) for more details!
