[![](http://actionablebooks.com/wp-content/uploads/2011/10/kindle.jpg "kindle")](http://actionablebooks.com/my-love-affair-with-kindle/kindle/)[Last month](http://actionablebooks.com/going-over-to-the-dark-side/) I wrote a blog post about “going over to the dark side”–buying an eBook reader.

As a bit of a refresher, I decided to invest in a Kobo. I didn’t do too much research, and really just decided to go with that brand because it’s the reader endorsed by Canada’s largest book chain, Chapters-Indigo-Coles.

I realized quite early, in fact within moments, that I had made a grave error. (Okay, maybe it wasn’t _that_ bad.) While I loved the sleekness of the device itself, Kobo’s library isn’t as comprehensive as Amazon’s Kindle library. There were books that I knew were for sale in the eBook format that just weren’t stocked by Kobo.

So rather than wallow in my disappointment, I decided to just buy a Kindle. I _love_ it. Not just because of the wider choice in books available at the click of a button (which is actually bad news if you’re someone like me…total kid in a candy store!), but because of the device itself. It has wireless internet access which connects to your home network. This means that you can download a book to your Kindle and literally be reading it moments later. With my Kobo I was forced to download the book to my computer’s library and then connect the Kobo to the computer with a USB cord in order to get the book on my reader.

I also love the fact that when your Kindle is in sleep mode, a stunning black and white image of an eminent author like Agatha Christie or Mark Twain is your screen saver. This also reflects the superior text quality of the Kindle; it truly does feel like you’re reading a book!

I’m thrilled that I upgraded to a Kindle. But my bad luck continued. Less than a month after receiving my new Kindle, Amazon rolled out the red carpet for their latest Kindles. Check them out [here](http://www.gizmodo.com.au/2011/09/this-is-amazons-new-us99-kindle-touch/), they’re pretty great!
